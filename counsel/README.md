# Lumen AI Counsel

Initial counsel roles:

1. Evidence
2. Provenance
3. Logic
4. Statistical Analysis
5. Privacy
6. Safety
7. Ethics
8. Legal Context
9. Definitions / Context
10. Devil's Advocate
11. Synthesis

Counsel are advisory components.

They do not possess unrestricted authority over user data or
high-impact actions.

The Devil's Advocate must identify:

- the challenged assumption;
- the objection;
- evidence supporting the objection;
- what evidence could resolve the dispute.

Counsel disagreement is preserved rather than artificially collapsed.
